# Rabbitsamurai: A Comprehensive Guide to Success

## Game Overview
### What is it?
Rabbitsamurai is a turn-based strategy game where players take on the role of a samurai warrior who must defend their village from invading rabbits.

# How to Play
### Controls and Objective
• Use your mouse to move your samurai warriors up, down, left, or right.
• Tap on an enemy rabbit to attack it.
• Your goal is to protect your villagers from all incoming rabbits while defending your own territory.

## Tips and Tricks for Success

### Defending the Village
- Keep a large army of samurai at home to defend against early invaders.
- Use traps and towers strategically to funnel rabbits into kill zones.
- Upgrade your samurai to increase their attack power and survivability.

### Outmaneuvering the Rabbits
- Use your territory's terrain to your advantage, such as by placing a trap near an enemy rabbit.
- Move your samurai quickly and efficiently to avoid being caught in a rabbit's line of fire.
- Communicate with your village (if you have one) about incoming rabbits and plan accordingly.

### Rabbit Patterns
- Be aware of the rabbits' movement patterns, such as their speed and aggression level.
- Use this knowledge to place traps or defend specific areas.

### Village Defense
- Upgrade your village's defenses (towers, traps, etc.) regularly to increase its survivability.
- Recruit villagers with high health and attack power to fight alongside you.
- Keep a healthy army at home to respond quickly to rabbit attacks.
